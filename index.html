How it actually goes

Three calls, in order.

Not a funnel — the real sequence, in the order our clients go through it, start to close.

01 — Week 1

Walk the shortlist

We tour four to six homes in a day, on foot, at the time of day you'd actually be living in them.

02 — Week 2

Write the offer

We price against comps we've verified in person, not an algorithm's estimate, and write terms that protect you.

03 — Weeks 3–6

Close and hand over keys

Inspection, appraisal, and closing, coordinated on one shared timeline so nothing surprises you at the table.
