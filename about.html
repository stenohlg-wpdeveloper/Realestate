How we started

Started at a kitchen table, still run that way.

Fieldstone began in 2011 with one agent and a folding table, tired of watching good houses get reduced to a photo carousel and a square-footage number. We built the brokerage around a simple idea: walk every home yourself before you ever describe it to someone else.

Fifteen years later, that's still the whole model. We take on fewer listings than most firms our size so that every one of them gets walked, measured, and written up by an agent who's actually stood in the room — not summarized from an MLS feed.

What we hold to

Three things we won't compromise on.

01

We walk it first

No agent describes a home they haven't personally toured — at the time of day a buyer would actually live in it.

02

We keep the roster short

We'd rather represent fewer homes well than list everything and coast on volume.

03

One agent, start to finish

You never get handed off to someone new mid-transaction. The person you meet is who closes with you.

How it actually goes

Three calls, in order.

Not a funnel — the real sequence, in the order our clients go through it, start to close.

01 — Week 1

Walk the shortlist

We tour four to six homes in a day, on foot, at the time of day you'd actually be living in them.

02 — Week 2

Write the offer

We price against comps we've verified in person, not an algorithm's estimate, and write terms that protect you.

03 — Weeks 3–6

Close and hand over keys

Inspection, appraisal, and closing, coordinated on one shared timeline so nothing surprises you at the table.
